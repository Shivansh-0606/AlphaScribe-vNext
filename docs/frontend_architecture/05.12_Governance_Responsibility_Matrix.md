## Purpose

Provide a **responsibility matrix** that makes explicit, for every governance activity, **who owns, reviews,
approves, and is a stakeholder** — so accountability for architectural decisions is unambiguous. It
consolidates (does not change) the ownership model in [05.7 Frontend Governance](05.7_Frontend_Governance.md)
and Constitution §6.5/§12.

## Scope

**In scope:** an activity-level Owner/Reviewer/Approver/Stakeholders matrix for architectural governance
activities. **Out of scope:** new governance rules (owned by [05.7](05.7_Frontend_Governance.md)),
implementation, and product/design governance (referenced, owned elsewhere).

## Architectural Principles

1. **Every activity has a single accountable Owner** (§4.10 explicitness); ambiguity is resolved by this
   matrix, then by [05.7](05.7_Frontend_Governance.md), then by CR.
2. **The CTO is the approval/freeze authority** for anything touching frozen scope (§12).
3. **Design owns design; Architecture owns structure; Engineering owns implementation** (§6.5) — the matrix
   never crosses these bright lines.

## Governance Responsibilities (the matrix)

Roles: **Owner** (drives/produces) · **Reviewer** (assesses before approval) · **Approver** (authorizes) ·
**Stakeholders** (consulted/informed). *FA = Frontend Architecture · FE = Frontend Engineering · DX =
Design/Experience Design · PM = Product.*

| Activity | Owner | Reviewer | Approver | Stakeholders |
|----------|-------|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Architecture changes** (to M1–M5) | FA | FA stewards + FE | **CTO** (via CR) | FE, DX, PM |
| **ADR creation** ([05.6](05.6_Architecture_Decision_Record_Index.md)) | FA (author) | FA stewards + FE | **CTO** (Accept) | FE |
| **Dependency introduction** ([05.5](05.5_Dependency_Guidelines.md)) | FE (proposer) | FA (lifecycle plan, a11y/perf/security fit) | **CTO** for core-stack; FA for in-stack | FE, Security |
| **Design system changes** (tokens/components/visuals) | **DX** | DX + FA (integration/consumption) | **CTO** (via CR) | FA, FE |
| **Cross-team reviews** (arch ↔ design ↔ backend) | FA (coordinates) | FA + DX + Backend | **CTO** where scope-affecting | FE, PM |
| **Architecture approval** (freeze a milestone) | FA (submits) | FA stewards | **CTO** | FE, DX, PM |
| **Documentation updates** ([05.2](05.2_Documentation_Standards.md)) | Author (FA/FE) | FA | FA (patch/minor); **CTO** (frozen-doc change via CR) | FE |
| **Breaking architectural changes** | FA | FA stewards + FE + affected DX/Backend | **CTO** (CR + ADR mandatory) | FE, DX, Backend, PM |
| **Deprecations** ([05.14](05.14_Architecture_Evolution_Strategy.md)) | FA | FA + FE | **CTO** for frozen items | FE |

**Reading the matrix:** the Owner produces the change/record; Reviewers must sign off before approval; the
Approver authorizes (the **CTO** for anything touching frozen scope or the approved stack); Stakeholders are
consulted and informed. Sign-offs are recorded ([05.7](05.7_Frontend_Governance.md)).

## Trade-offs

- **Explicit role assignment vs. flexibility:** naming a single Owner/Approver per activity reduces
  ambiguity at the cost of some flexibility; clarity wins for governance. Accepted.
- **A separate matrix vs. prose in 05.7:** a matrix is faster to consult than prose, at a small duplication
  cost mitigated by linking to [05.7](05.7_Frontend_Governance.md) as the authority. Accepted.

## Risks

- **Diffusion of responsibility** (no clear owner). *Mitigation:* single-Owner rule (Principle 1) + this
  matrix.
- **Matrix drifting from [05.7](05.7_Frontend_Governance.md).** *Mitigation:* 05.7 is authoritative; the
  matrix consolidates it and is updated via governance when it changes.
- **Bypassing the Approver under pressure.** *Mitigation:* approval as a merge/freeze gate (§12, [05.9](05.9_Architecture_Review_Checklist.md)).

## Future Evolution

- New activities or roles (e.g. a security reviewer, additional stewards) are added as rows/columns as the
  team scales, via governance.
- If ownership boundaries ever change, [05.7](05.7_Frontend_Governance.md) is updated first, then this matrix.
