# Vision Statement

AlphaScribe is an AI-native Equity Research Workspace designed to help investors understand, analyze, compare, and monitor publicly traded companies without spending hours searching through financial filings, earnings calls, news articles, and financial statements.

Instead of acting as another AI chatbot, AlphaScribe combines a modern research workspace with an intelligent financial copilot that assists users throughout the entire investment research process.

Our goal is to provide institutional-quality equity research in a simple, beautiful, and accessible experience.

---

# Mission

Empower every investor with the ability to perform professional-grade equity research using artificial intelligence, trusted financial data, and intuitive design.

---

# Problem Statement

Today's investment research process is fragmented and time-consuming.

Investors often spend hours:

- Searching SEC filings
- Reading annual and quarterly reports
- Watching earnings calls
- Comparing companies
- Collecting financial metrics
- Understanding risks
- Creating research notes

The information exists, but it is scattered across multiple platforms.

Professional tools like Bloomberg Terminal and AlphaSense provide excellent capabilities but are expensive and often overwhelming for individual investors.

General-purpose AI tools can answer financial questions but lack structured workflows, reliable financial context, and dedicated research capabilities.

---

# Our Solution

AlphaScribe provides a single AI-powered workspace where investors can:

- Search companies
- Analyze financial statements
- Explore historical performance
- Compare competitors
- Read AI-generated summaries
- Ask contextual financial questions
- Generate professional research reports
- Save research for future reference

The platform combines structured financial data, AI reasoning, and interactive visualizations into one seamless experience.

---

# Product Philosophy

AlphaScribe is not "ChatGPT for Finance."

AlphaScribe is an AI-powered Equity Research Workspace where conversational intelligence enhances every workflow instead of replacing it.

AI is the engine behind the platform—not the entire product.

# Value Proposition

AlphaScribe enables investors to:

- Research companies faster
- Understand businesses more deeply
- Compare companies more effectively
- Reduce manual research effort
- Make better-informed investment decisions

without sacrificing transparency or usability.

AlphaScribe's competitive advantage is not simply AI-powered research, but AI
research that earns trust. Every insight is:

- **Grounded** in primary sources rather than generated from open-ended memory
- **Explainable**, so the user always understands how a conclusion was reached
- **Traceable** back to the evidence behind it, inspectable down to the citation

This trust-first positioning—grounded, explainable, and source-traceable AI—is
what separates AlphaScribe from general-purpose AI tools and is the differentiator
the rest of this documentation set is built to protect.

---

# Core Product Principles

Every feature should follow these principles:

1. Simplicity first.
2. Beautiful user experience.
3. AI should enhance—not complicate—the workflow.
4. Every insight should be explainable.
5. Trust is more important than speed.
6. Professional capabilities should be accessible to everyone.

# Pricing Philosophy

AlphaScribe supports two usage models.

## Bring Your Own API Key (BYOK)

Users can connect their own AI provider API keys.

Benefits:

- Lower cost
- Provider flexibility
- Greater control

## Managed AI

Users can subscribe to AlphaScribe-managed AI services without configuring their own API keys.

Benefits:

- Zero setup
- Simple onboarding
- Better user experience

# Competitive Landscape

Primary competitors:

- AlphaSense
- Bloomberg Terminal
- FinChat

Rather than copying these platforms, AlphaScribe aims to deliver a more intuitive, AI-native research experience with a significantly lower barrier to entry.

---

# MVP Goals

The first release should enable users to:

- Search and explore public companies
- Chat with an AI financial copilot
- Analyze SEC filings
- Generate AI summaries
- Compare companies
- View key financial metrics
- Export research reports

The MVP should solve one problem exceptionally well:

"Reduce hours of manual equity research into minutes."

---

# Out of Scope (MVP)

The following features are intentionally excluded from the first version:

- Live trading
- Portfolio execution
- Brokerage integrations
- Options analytics
- Cryptocurrency research
- Social investing
- Community features

These may be considered after achieving product-market fit.

---

# Success Metrics

The product will be considered successful when users can:

- Complete company research significantly faster than traditional methods
- Trust AI-generated insights
- Return to AlphaScribe as their primary research workspace
- Recommend the platform to other investors
